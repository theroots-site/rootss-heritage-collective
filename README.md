# Rootss Heritage Collective

Create a landing page for my brand the rootSS which is a Indian heritage brand built for Rootss is created for a discerning, design-conscious audience that appreciates India's rich artistic heritage and the beauty of handmade creations. Our audience includes art enthusiasts, collectors, interior designers, architects, home décor lovers, fashion-conscious individuals, luxury lifestyle seekers and conscious consumers who value authenticity, craftsmanship and timeless aesthetics.

We cater to a niche, aspirational market seeking something beyond mass-produced products and conventional luxury. They are drawn to traditional Indian crafts such as Dokra metal art, terracotta, tribal art, handcrafted sculptures, indigenous artefacts and unique junk jewellery that celebrate generations of artistic knowledge and cultural identity.

Our audience also includes individuals looking to beautify, renovate or transform their homes and personal spaces with distinctive décor, artistic accents, statement pieces and culturally inspired designs. From home makeovers and interior styling to unique fashion accessories and handcrafted jewellery, Rootss appeals to those who love adding character, creativity and individuality to their surroundings and personal style.

Our customers appreciate the stories behind every creation and recognise the skill, dedication and creativity of the artisans. They believe in preserving India's traditional crafts, supporting artisan communities and encouraging sustainable livelihoods through conscious purchasing.

Rootss bridges traditional craftsmanship with contemporary lifestyles, making authentic Indian art and thoughtfully curated lifestyle products accessible to those who seek meaningful, distinctive and culturally rooted choices.

Positioned in the affordable luxury segment, Rootss appeals to customers who desire exclusivity, artistic value and refined aesthetics without inaccessible luxury pricing.
